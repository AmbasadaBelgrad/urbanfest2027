Roundtable on urban volunteering

This city needs us

Bringing people and initiatives together to work on useful projects for Belgrade.

9 January 2027
Prostor Miljenko Dereta, Dobračina 55, Belgrade

From individual initiatives to shared projects

Belgrade’s Russian-speaking community already takes part in city life: collecting litter, planting trees and helping animals. We will discuss how to connect this experience and make our collective work more sustained and visible.

The conversation follows the life of an urban project, from an idea and a plan to working with city authorities and reaching Serbian media. Community leaders, experts, volunteers and residents who want to get involved are welcome.

We want to leave the conversation with ideas for practical projects we can take on together.

The focus is on Belgrade’s practical needs, the experience of local initiatives and finding people ready to join forces. Bring your experience, questions or an idea for the city.
