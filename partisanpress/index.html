Partisanpress at UrbanFest

Words, ink, paper

Find a short phrase and print a postcard on a hand-operated Adana letterpress with Sergey Besov.

9 January 2027
Prostor Miljenko Dereta, Dobračina 55, Belgrade

From a phrase to a print

What word would you like to leave on paper? In the Partisanpress workshop, we will search for words we need, and words we do not, put together short phrases and turn them into postcards.

Letterpress printing brings together type, ink and paper. Sergey will demonstrate the process on an Adana press, from preparing the printing form to making an impression.

“The street and people’s homes are our gallery”

Alongside postcard printing, we are preparing a conversation with Sergey about Partisanpress. An exhibition and a print for Belgrade are also planned. Details of these parts of the programme will be announced separately.

Sergey Besov

Sergey works with letterpress printing through the Demon Press workshop and Partisanpress. At the festival, he will talk about the project and demonstrate postcard printing on an Adana press.
