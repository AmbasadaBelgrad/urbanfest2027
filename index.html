our story

six editions before 2027

UrbanFest started in 2020. Before the move to Serbia, the team organised three editions in Russia: one in Moscow and two in St Petersburg. In Belgrade, UrbanFest took place in January 2024, September 2024 and January 2026. UrbanFest 2027 will be the seventh edition overall and the fourth in Belgrade.

about UrbanFest

cities are easier to change when we understand them

UrbanFest is a free independent festival about how cities work and how they can work better.

We bring together urbanists, architects, researchers, designers, transport experts and residents for talks, workshops and conversations rooted in Belgrade and connected to experience from other cities. The first Belgrade edition in 2024 also helped spark Ambasada za urbanizam, which now develops research, public-space, mobility and urban-heritage projects throughout the year.

the people behind UrbanFest

Ambasada za urbanizam

UrbanFest is organised in Belgrade by Ambasada za urbanizam, an interdisciplinary civic initiative working on urban research, public space, mobility, heritage and participation. Pulse Urban Alliance (PULS) provides the legal organisational framework in Serbia.
